Giant Chocolate Chip Cookie

This giant chocolate chip cookie is perfect for parties and a fun alternative to a traditional birthday cake.

Ingredients

  • 6 Tbsp butter or margarine (3/4 stick), softened
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1–2/3 cups premium chocolate chips

Step-By-Step Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a circular pizza pan carefully with foil wrapper sheets.
  2. Beat together the butter and sugars until fluffy, then whisk in the egg and vanilla extract.
  3. Whisk flour and baking soda in a separate bowl, then combine gently with wet ingredients.
  4. Spread the thick cookie cookie dough batter evenly across your prepared pizza baking pan surface.
  5. Bake for 18–22 minutes until structural edges are golden crisp and center is set.
  6. Cool completely inside the pan frame, slice cleanly like a pizza cake, and serve up.
